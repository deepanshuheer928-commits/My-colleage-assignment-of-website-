  Got Questions?

Frequently Asked

How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days within the US. Express shipping (1–2 days) is available at checkout for a small additional fee. All orders placed before 2 PM EST are dispatched the same business day.
What is your return policy?
We offer a hassle-free 30-day return policy on all orders. If you're not fully satisfied with your purchase, simply send the shoes back in their original condition and packaging, and we'll issue a full refund within 5–7 business days — no questions asked.
How do I know which size to order?
Bolt shoes generally fit true to US sizing. If you're between sizes, we recommend going half a size up for the Blaze X1 running shoe for extra toe room during runs. The Cloud Drift and Urban Street fit standard US sizing accurately. When in doubt, go half a size up.
Are Bolt shoes suitable for wide feet?
Yes! All Bolt models are designed with a slightly generous toe box to accommodate wider feet comfortably. The Cloud Drift has the widest natural fit across our range. For very wide feet, we suggest sizing up by half a size for the best comfort.
Can I cancel or change my order after placing it?
Orders can be modified or cancelled within 2 hours of placement. After that, the order enters our fulfilment process and changes may not be possible. Contact us immediately at deepanshuheer928@gmail.com and we'll do our best to assist you before dispatch.
Do you ship internationally?
Yes, we ship to over 40 countries worldwide. International shipping typically takes 7–14 business days. Duties and taxes may apply depending on your country's import regulations — these are the responsibility of the customer. Shipping rates are calculated at checkout.
Are the shoes waterproof?
The Blaze X1 and Cloud Drift feature water-resistant mesh uppers that can handle light rain and damp surfaces. The Urban Street suede model is not water-resistant and should be treated with a suede protector spray before outdoor use. None of our current models are fully waterproof.
